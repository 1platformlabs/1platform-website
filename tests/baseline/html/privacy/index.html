1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following categories:

Information you provide directly

  • Account information: Name, email address, company name, and password when you register
  • Billing information: Payment method details (processed securely; we never store card data on our servers)
  • Content data: Text, keywords, URLs, images, and other content you submit for processing through our services
  • Communication data: Messages you send to our support team or through feedback channels

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: API calls, feature usage, timestamps, and session duration
  • Device information: Browser type, operating system, screen resolution, and device identifiers
  • Log data: IP addresses, referring URLs, pages visited, and error logs
  • Cookies and similar technologies: As described in our Cookie Policy

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Process transactions and send related information (confirmations, invoices)
  • Send technical notices, updates, security alerts, and support messages
  • Respond to your comments, questions, and support requests
  • Monitor and analyze usage trends to improve user experience
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activity
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Providers

To deliver our unified platform capabilities, we share specific categories of data with the following third-party service providers:

AI processing providers

We use OpenAI for AI-powered content generation, comment creation, and text processing. Content you submit for AI processing (keywords, prompts, article parameters) is sent to OpenAI's API for processing. OpenAI's data usage policies apply to this processing.

Search engine APIs

We use ValueSerp to retrieve search engine results data for keyword research and SERP analysis. Search queries and keyword data are transmitted to ValueSerp for processing.

Search data providers

Search volume, keyword difficulty, and related keyword data may be sourced through third-party search data APIs to provide comprehensive keyword intelligence.

Image sourcing providers

We use Pixabay and Pexels to source royalty-free images for your content. Search queries related to image requests are transmitted to these providers.

Link building providers

We use Publisuites for link building and sponsored content placement services. Website URLs, anchor text, and campaign parameters are shared as necessary to execute link building campaigns.

Payment processors

We use Migo as our payment processing provider in Guatemala. When you make a payment, your transaction details (amount, currency, payment method) are securely transmitted to Migo for processing. We never store your complete credit card information on our servers.

Invoicing providers

We use TribuTax as our electronic invoicing (FEL) provider for generating legally compliant invoices in Guatemala. Your billing information (name, tax ID, address) is shared with TribuTax to generate and certify electronic invoices as required by Guatemalan tax law.

Delivery and courier providers

When you dispatch an order through a contracted courier service rather than your own staff, the delivery details you supply are transmitted to that courier so it can collect and deliver the parcel: your buyer's name, phone number, delivery address and, where you provide them, its coordinates. Deliveries you fulfil with your own staff are not shared with anyone. The public tracking link your buyer opens identifies the shipment and does not disclose the courier.

Advertising networks

If you connect an advertising account to run paid campaigns, you grant us management scopes over the pages and campaigns you select on that network so we can publish and control them on your behalf. The campaign material you create, the budget you assign and the performance figures the network reports back are exchanged with that network. This happens only for accounts you connect yourself, and we never sell your data to advertisers.

Card-present acquiring providers

If you request a terminal to accept card-present payments, the supporting documentation you upload is shared with the acquiring provider that reviews the request: your business's legal documents and its tax identification. The provider uses them to assess and resolve your request and retains them as financial regulation requires.

Other sharing

We may also share your information:

  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of 1Platform Labs, our users, or the public
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with prior notice)
  • With your consent or at your direction

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers with industry-standard protections, including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest
  • Token-based authentication with role separation
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel only

While we implement robust security measures, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data according to the following schedule:

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active and for 30 days after deletion
  • Generated content: Retained for up to 90 days after account deletion
  • API logs: Retained for 90 days for debugging and analytics
  • Billing records: Retained as required by applicable tax and accounting laws
  • Anonymized analytics: May be retained indefinitely in aggregate form

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data for certain purposes
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@1platform.pro. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

7. Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. For detailed information about the cookies we use and how to manage them, please see our Cookie Policy.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child without parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. When we transfer data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on our website and updating the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact Information

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@1platform.pro
  • Website: https://1platform.pro
