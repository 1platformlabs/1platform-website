1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to site owners.

Cookies can be "persistent" (remaining on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser). They can be set by the website you are visiting ("first-party cookies") or by third-party services embedded in the website ("third-party cookies").

2. Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the operation of our website and services. They enable core functionality such as security, session management, and accessibility. Without these cookies, the Service cannot function properly.

  • Session cookies: Maintain your authenticated session while using the dashboard or API
  • Security cookies: Help detect and prevent fraudulent activity and unauthorized access
  • Preference cookies: Remember your essential settings (e.g., language preference, accepted cookie consent)

These cookies cannot be disabled as they are required for the Service to function.

3. Analytics Cookies

We use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our website. This helps us improve the user experience, identify issues, and measure the effectiveness of our content. Analytics data is collected in aggregate and does not personally identify individual users.

Information collected through analytics cookies includes:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Navigation paths through the website
  • Browser and device type
  • Referring website or campaign source
  • General geographic location (country/city level)

4. Preference Cookies

Preference cookies allow our website to remember choices you have made in the past, such as:

  • Your preferred language (Spanish or English)
  • Dashboard layout and display preferences
  • Whether you have dismissed informational banners or notifications

These cookies improve your experience by personalizing the Service to your preferences. Disabling them may result in a less tailored experience.

5. Advertising Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We do not run ads on 1platform.pro, sell your data to advertisers, or use cookies to build advertising profiles. Your browsing activity on our website is never shared with ad networks or marketing platforms.

This is a statement about the cookies this site sets, not about what the platform sells. 1Platform does offer advertising campaign management as a product capability: inside the dashboard you can connect an advertising account of your own and run paid campaigns with it. That is something you opt into as a customer, it runs on the account you connect, and nothing on this website tracks you for it.

6. How to Manage Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

Browser settings

Most browsers allow you to view, manage, and delete cookies through their settings. The process varies by browser:

  • Chrome: Settings / Privacy and Security / Cookies and Other Site Data
  • Firefox: Settings / Privacy & Security / Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences / Privacy / Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings / Cookies and Site Permissions / Manage and Delete Cookies

Opting out of analytics

If you wish to opt out of analytics tracking, you can:

  • Use your browser's "Do Not Track" setting
  • Install browser extensions that block analytics scripts
  • Clear your cookies regularly through your browser settings

Impact of disabling cookies

Please note that disabling essential cookies may prevent you from using certain features of the Service, including logging in to your account and accessing the API dashboard.
