Before you start

Frequently asked questions.

Open your account
What can I connect from my account?

Your online store, payments, invoicing and deliveries work together. You can also add a website, content and advertising to help customers find your business.

Can I charge without an online store?

Yes. Share a payment link with your customer. The link opens a checkout, and the person paying does not need a 1Platform account.

Is the invoice issued automatically?

Yes, when electronic invoicing is configured for your business. Electronic invoicing currently supports Guatemala; availability and setup depend on the jurisdiction.

How are rates calculated?

Billable services use your prepaid USD balance at the rates set for your account. Payment processing and invoicing require a quote for your operation. Review pricing for the complete model.
